Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the team! Your first week should include: setting up your
laptop and company email, completing HR paperwork, meeting your manager
and team members, and reviewing the employee handbook. IT will provision
your accounts within 24 hours of your start date. All new hires must
complete the mandatory security-awareness training within the first 7
days. Benefits enrollment (health insurance, retirement plan) must be
completed within 30 days of your start date or you will be auto-enrolled
in the default plan.
